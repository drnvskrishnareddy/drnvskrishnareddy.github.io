Thoracic Aortic Z-Score Calculator

Age-adaptive reference: Quezada et al. (Turner syndrome cohort, <15y, 8 aortic levels) below age 15; Campens et al. (general population, ≥15y, sinuses of Valsalva & ascending aorta) from age 15 onward. BSA by Haycock throughout.

Open source

DR. NVS KRISHNA REDDY, is a DM Endocrinology Resident at All India Institute of Medical Sciences, Bhopal.

This is an Open Source Software developed for calculation of Z scores in Turners Syndrome. The calculator automatically switches between the normograms to be used.

— Enter an age to see which reference equation set applies.

Patient parameters

Years. <15y → Quezada (Turner cohort); ≥15y → Campens (general population).
Used by the Campens (≥15y) equations, which are sex-specific. The Quezada equations (<15y) were derived in a Turner syndrome — i.e. all-female — cohort.
Enter age, sex, height, weight, the aortic level, and the measured diameter, then calculate.
References. Quezada E, Lapidus J, Shaughnessy R, Chen Z, Silberbach M. Aortic dimensions in Turner syndrome. Am J Med Genet A 2015;167A:2527–2532 (Table II — annulus, aortic root, ascending aorta, sinotubular junction, proximal/distal transverse arch, isthmus, descending aorta; BSA + BSA² model on the √diameter scale; applied here for age <15y). Campens L, Demulier L, De Groote K, et al. Reference values for echocardiographic assessment of the diameter of the aortic root and ascending aorta spanning all age categories. Am J Cardiol 2014;114:914–920 (Table 2 — sinuses of Valsalva and ascending aorta; Haycock-BSA + age + sex model on the log₁₀ diameter scale; applied here for age ≥15y). Body surface area by Haycock GB, Schwartz GJ, Wisotsky DH. J Pediatr 1978;93:62–66.
