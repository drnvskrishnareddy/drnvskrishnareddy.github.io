Height centiles & SDS, aortic dilatation risk assessment, and per-site aortic Z-scores — all in one place.

Open source

DR. NVS KRISHNA REDDY, is a DM Endocrinology Resident at All India Institute of Medical Sciences, Bhopal.

This is an Open Source Software developed for calculation of Centiles, SD scores, and aortic dilatation risk in Turner syndrome. The calculator automatically switches between the nomograms to be used.

01

Height

Centile & SDS for height — choose General (IAP, boys or girls) or Turner-specific (TS-Indian & Lyon cohorts).

02

Aortic Dilatation Assessment

ASI, AHI, and Z-score with age- and risk-factor-adjusted surveillance recommendation.

03

Aortic Indices

Z-score by aortic level (annulus, root, ascending aorta, arch segments, etc.), age-adaptive reference.
