Jal Santulan privacy policy

Last updated 10 November 2026 · Jal Santulan home

Who we are

Jal Santulan is a water-balance and sodium monitoring app for people with AVP deficiency (diabetes insipidus), with a dashboard for their endocrine team. It is developed by Aravind Kumar Eligeti and Dr. NVS Krishna Reddy. Contact: sakhisamachar.app@gmail.com.

What the app records

  • The details the patient enters at sign-up: name, whether the patient is a child, days since surgery, weight, diagnosis, other hormone tablets and the desmopressin dose.
  • Readings the patient or the team enter: urine and drink volumes, weight, sodium and glucose results, symptoms, doses taken, and dose changes.
  • The patient's Google account email and name, to show whose record it is.
  • Jug photos used to read a volume are read on the phone and deleted; they are not saved to Google Drive.

Where it is kept

  • In a folder called “Jal Santulan” in the patient's own Google Drive: one record file and a spreadsheet copy of the readings. There is no Jal Santulan server or database, and the developers cannot see the records.
  • A working copy is kept in the browser on the patient's phone, so the app works without internet and is locked with a PIN. On the dashboard computer the copy is cleared when the browser tab is closed.

Who can see it

  • The endocrine unit's Google account, which the patient shares the record with after agreeing in the app. The unit can add readings, set the prescription and acknowledge alerts.
  • Any family member or carer the patient chooses to share with from the app.
  • Nobody else. The records are not sold, used for advertising, or shared with any hospital information system.

The patient can stop sharing at any time by removing the unit or carer from the “Jal Santulan” folder in Google Drive, and can delete the record by deleting that folder.

Google account access

Jal Santulan asks Google for your email address, your name, and the Google Drive permission “See, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app”. It cannot see any other files in your Drive. The dashboard uses Google Picker so the unit can choose the records patients have shared with it.

Jal Santulan's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Consent and children

Records are shared with the unit only after the patient (or, for a child, the parent or guardian) agrees in the app, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Research use is optional and off unless the patient switches it on.

Medical disclaimer

Jal Santulan is decision support for the patient and their endocrine team. It is not a certified medical device and does not replace medical advice. In an emergency, call 108 or go to the nearest emergency department.
