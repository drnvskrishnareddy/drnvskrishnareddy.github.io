Jal Santulan

जल संतुलन

Daily water balance and sodium monitoring for people with AVP deficiency (diabetes insipidus), after pituitary or hypothalamic surgery and long term. Patients log urine, drinks, weight, sodium and desmopressin doses; the endocrine team sees risk-sorted alerts.

Patient app

Sign in with your own Google account. Best on a phone: open in Chrome and use “Add to Home screen” to keep it like an app.

Where the records are kept

  • Each patient's record is a file in a “Jal Santulan” folder in the patient's own Google Drive. There is no Jal Santulan server or database.
  • After the patient agrees, the record is shared with the unit's Google account. A family member or carer can be added from the app.
  • Alerts are worked out in the app and on the dashboard each time they are opened. No SMS is sent.
  • Jal Santulan can open only the files it created or that the user chose for it in Google Drive.
